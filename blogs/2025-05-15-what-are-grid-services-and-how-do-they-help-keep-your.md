You might have heard the term "grid services" mentioned when talking about solar batteries, especially with smart systems like Reposit. But what exactly are they, and why should you care? Think of grid services as the behind-the-scenes work that keeps Australia's electricity network stable and reliable, ensuring the power stays on in your home.
