# Reposit Power (reposit-power)

Reposit Power is an Australian home-energy technology company founded in 2012 and headquartered in Canberra, ACT, that builds the Reposit Controller — a local control device and cloud platform that sits on top of a household's solar, battery and meter and trades that stored energy into the National Electricity Market on the owner's behalf, paying the household back as GridCredits and underwriting the result with its No Bill guarantee. It is not a retailer, a distributor or a meter provider; it sits one layer above them in the Australian energy value chain as a distributed-energy-resource aggregator and virtual power plant operator, selling through a national network of solar installers and partnering with retailers and network businesses who dispatch and curtail its fleet.

Its API posture is the opposite of the retailer pattern and needs stating plainly. Reposit is **not** a designated Consumer Data Right energy data holder — it does not appear among the 84 energy data-holder brands on the ACCC CDR Register, and it is not an accredited data recipient — so the Australian statutory energy mandate simply does not reach it, and the company publishes no CDR page at all. What it does publish, entirely voluntarily and with no obligation compelling it, is two real, downloadable, anonymously readable OpenAPI contracts behind two live Swagger UI pages. Every operational endpoint on both APIs returned 401 anonymously — the contracts are open, the data is not.

## APIs

### Reposit Customer API

Reposit's own documented REST API for the owner of a Reposit-controlled home energy system, described by a verbatim OpenAPI 3.0.3 contract served anonymously at `https://api.repositpower.com/spec/`. Eleven operations: log in, refresh, list the userkeys attached to the account, and read one deployment's hardware components, battery capacity, historical solar generation, battery-inverter real power, house consumption, battery state of charge in kWh, real power at the grid connection, and GridCredits earned in dollars. Authentication is a bearer JWT obtained by POSTing Reposit account credentials — a developer who is not a Reposit customer cannot obtain a token.

- **Human URL:** [https://api.repositpower.com/docs/](https://api.repositpower.com/docs/)
- **Base URL:** `https://api.repositpower.com/`

### Reposit Power Market API

The larger of Reposit's two published contracts, and the one that actually controls hardware. A verbatim OpenAPI 3.0.1 document served anonymously at `https://marketapi.repositpower.com/docs/spec/` and rendered as "Reposit Power Fleet API". Twenty-eight operations across twenty-two paths, tagged both by subject — Node, Power Station, Data, Curtailment, Dispatch, Users — and by the audience allowed to call them: Network, Retailer, Installer, Customer. This is the virtual-power-plant control plane: a network or retailer partner can curtail or dispatch the exports of real Australian households through it. Authentication is a non-expiring, IP-whitelistable API key generated by hand inside the Reposit Fleet web app, so the real gate is a commercial relationship.

- **Human URL:** [https://marketapi.repositpower.com/docs/](https://marketapi.repositpower.com/docs/)
- **Base URL:** `https://marketapi.repositpower.com/`

## Mandate, Standards and Access

- **Mandate regime:** none. The Consumer Data Right energy designation attaches to retailers, distribution network service providers and AEMO. Reposit is a DER aggregator sitting above all three, so the mandate stops one layer below it.
- **Mandate status:** not-applicable — not designated-not-live. Verified on 2026-07-27 against the public ACCC CDR Register: the energy data-holder brand summary returned 84 brands and zero matches for "reposit", and the all-data-recipients endpoint (x-v: 3) returned zero matches. `/cdr`, `/consumer-data-right` and `/data-sharing` on repositpower.com all 404.
- **Data standard:** no standard reference found. No Green Button/ESPI, no Consumer Data Standards, no IEEE 2030.5/CSIP-AUS, no OpenADR, no OCPP/OCPI, no IEC CIM. The only sector vocabulary is the Australian National Meter Identifier (`nmi`).
- **Consumer data API:** yes, via the account holder's own credentials — no OAuth flow, no scopes, no consent framework.
- **Open market data:** none. Every probed operational endpoint returned HTTP 401.
- **Access gate:** customer-account-required for the Customer API; partner-only (a Reposit Fleet account) for the Market API. Neither is self-serve.
- **Auth model:** HTTP bearer JWT from `/v2/auth/login/` on the Customer API; a non-expiring, optionally IP-whitelisted API key sent as `Authorization: Bearer` on the Market API. No OpenID Connect discovery document is served on any Reposit host.

The full probe log, register evidence and governance findings are in [review.yml](review.yml).
